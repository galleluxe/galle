## The Art of Maceration

At GALLE, patience is not a virtue — it is a technique.

Every fragrance we create undergoes a minimum of six weeks of maceration: the slow, deliberate steeping of raw aromatic materials in a base of high-grade alcohol. During this period, the volatile molecules of each ingredient intertwine, negotiate, and ultimately harmonize into something greater than the sum of their parts.

### Why Time Matters

A freshly blended formula is often a cacophony. The sharp aldehydes push against the earthiness of vetiver; the citrus top notes overwhelm the subtle heliotrope in the heart. Only with time — and the gradual oxidation that maceration permits — does a fragrance find its voice.

We store our macerating blends in glass vessels, sealed from light and fluctuating temperatures, in a dedicated aging room in our Bangalore facility. Temperature is held at a precise 16°C.

### The Nose Knows

Our perfumer, Kavya Reddy, revisits each blend every fortnight. She evaluates not just the scent but the *silage* — how the fragrance trails in the air, the persistence it carries on skin warmed by the body's own heat.

"A fragrance reveals its character slowly," she says. "The first week you smell the potential. By the sixth week, you smell the truth."

### What Maceration Produces

- **Greater depth**: Base notes emerge fully rather than appearing only on dry-down.
- **Smoother transitions**: The journey from top through heart to base becomes seamless.
- **Improved longevity**: Molecular bonding strengthens tenacity on skin.

Every bottle of GALLE that reaches you has earned its age.
